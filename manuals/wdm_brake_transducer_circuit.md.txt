### 2. SCOPE AND PURPOSE

This data module defines the complete wiring architecture, conductor routing,
connector specification, and pin-out assignment for the analog pressure telemetry
circuit originating at the Brake Pressure Transducer (FIN: 12MG) in Zone 710,
routed via harness W-32-MG100-SERIES to the Brake System Control Unit Channel 1
(BSCU-1) installed at Main Avionics Rack 8VU, and return-grounded at Ground
Block GD-02 (Frame 24, Zone 120).

This document provides the authoritative pin-to-pin wiring source for:
- Connector maintenance, continuity verification, and pin extraction/insertion.
- Fault isolation of signal anomalies on the 1.0 V–5.0 V analog telemetry loop.
- EMI shielding integrity verification at bulkhead termination points.
- Replacement of harness sub-segments identified in AWM Chapter 20-54.

### 3. SYSTEM SCHEMATIC SUMMARY

#### 3.1 Signal Chain Architecture

The brake pressure telemetry loop is a single-ended analog voltage signal
operating in the range **1.0 VDC (0 bar) to 5.0 VDC (350 bar)**, generated
by the piezo-resistive bridge of the Brake Pressure Transducer FIN 12MG
(Zone 710, Left Main Landing Gear Strut Lower Forward Segment).

#### 3.2 EMI Shield Architecture

The twisted shielded pair carrying Pin C (signal) and Pin B (ground) is enclosed
in an outer braid shield (Wire Identifier: W-32-MG105-D-SH). Shield termination
follows AWM Chapter 20-54 single-point ground discipline:

- **Transducer end (Zone 710):** Shield is NOT grounded. Braid is floating,
  terminated with insulating heat-shrink sleeve at the CN-LG32 backshell entry.
- **Bulkhead end (Frame 24):** Shield braid drain wire terminates at CN-LG32
  Pin D, which routes via harness to Ground Block GD-02 at Frame 24.
- **Single-point grounding** eliminates ground loop interference on the
  1.0 V–5.0 V low-level analog signal path.
- **Shield coverage:** Minimum 90% optical coverage per MIL-W-22759 braid
  specification. Verify coverage before harness installation using continuity
  check from braid-to-Pin D: target resistance < 0.5 Ω end-to-end.

### 8. WIRING CONTINUITY AND ISOLATION TEST PROCEDURE

> ℹ️ **NOTE:** Perform the following tests with CB-10MG OPEN (pulled), aircraft
> power OFF, and connectors CN-LG32 and J8VU-32 both unmated. Use calibrated
> Digital Multimeter T11 (CAT III, 600 V) and Megger tester (500 VDC output)
> for isolation tests.

| Test | Pin(s) Tested          | Test Method                         | Acceptable Result                     | Reject If                         |
|------|------------------------|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| T01  | Pin A — continuity     | DMM resistance, Pin A to CB-10MG output stud | < 0.8 Ω end-to-end          | ≥ 0.8 Ω (open or high resistance)|
| T02  | Pin B — continuity     | DMM resistance, Pin B to GD-02 Lug 7 | < 0.8 Ω end-to-end                  | ≥ 0.8 Ω                           |
| T03  | Pin C — continuity     | DMM resistance, Pin C to J8VU-32 Pin 12 | < 1.2 Ω end-to-end             | ≥ 1.2 Ω                           |
| T04  | Pin D — shield continuity | DMM resistance, Pin D to GD-02 Lug 8 | < 0.5 Ω end-to-end             | ≥ 0.5 Ω                           |
| T05  | Pin A to Pin B         | Megger 500 VDC, pin-to-pin isolation | ≥ 10 MΩ                              | < 10 MΩ (insulation breakdown)   |
| T06  | Pin A to Pin C         | Megger 500 VDC, pin-to-pin isolation | ≥ 10 MΩ                              | < 10 MΩ                           |
| T07  | Pin A to Pin D         | Megger 500 VDC, pin-to-pin isolation | ≥ 10 MΩ                              | < 10 MΩ                           |
| T08  | Pin B to Pin C         | Megger 500 VDC, pin-to-pin isolation | ≥ 10 MΩ                              | < 10 MΩ                           |
| T09  | Pin D float (Zone 710) | Megger 500 VDC, Pin D braid to airframe, Zone 710 | ≥ 1 MΩ          | < 1 MΩ (shield grounded at wrong end — ground loop fault) |
| T10  | GD-02 ground bond      | DMM resistance, GD-02 Lug bus to Frame 24 structure | < 2.5 mΩ       | ≥ 2.5 mΩ (degraded airframe bond)|

### 9. FAULT SIGNATURES AND WIRING CROSS-REFERENCE

| Fault Code              | Signal Condition at BSCU-1 AV-12 | Probable Wiring Cause                      | Isolation Starting Point               |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| `CMS-FAULT-32-42-E12`   | Pin C < 0.8 VDC sustained        | Open circuit — Pin A (no excitation) or Pin C signal wire | Test T01 (Pin A continuity) first     |
