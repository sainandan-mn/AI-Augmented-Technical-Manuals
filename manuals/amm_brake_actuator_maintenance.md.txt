### 2. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

> ⚠️ **WARNING:** The Blue Hydraulic System operates at a nominal pressure of
> 3,000 PSI (206 bar). Residual trapped pressure in local brake lines may reach
> 1,500 PSI (103 bar) even after system-level depressurization. Contact with
> pressurized hydraulic fluid at this level constitutes a HIGH-PRESSURE INJECTION
> HAZARD capable of penetrating skin and causing severe tissue necrosis, systemic
> toxicity, or death. Do not loosen any hydraulic union, coupling, or bleed fitting
> until system depressurization is confirmed at local test point TP-32A (pressure
> ≤ 50 PSI / 3.4 bar as indicated on GSE-HYD-4200 gauge). Always direct drain
> flow away from personnel. Wear face shield (EN166 rated), chemical-resistant
> gloves (EN374 rated), and hydraulic-fluid-resistant coveralls before
> disconnecting any fluid-carrying line.

> 🛑 **CAUTION:** The Main Brake Actuator Module (FIN: 10MG-A) contains internal
> LVDT position transducers and pressure-sensing microcircuitry sensitive to
> Electrostatic Discharge (ESD). Discharge to components at or above 100V may
> cause latent or immediate failure of transducer signal processing circuits
> without visible external damage. Before handling the actuator assembly or
> its electrical connectors, don a wrist strap bonded to airframe earth
> (resistance verified < 1 MΩ per AMM Std. 20-93-00). Store removed unit
> immediately in approved ESD-protective packaging (pink polyethylene
> antistatic bag minimum; conductive hard-shell case preferred for transit).
> Do not place unit on unprotected metal surfaces or synthetic-material
> workbenches.

> ℹ️ **NOTE:** This task requires a minimum crew of two (2) certified
> maintenance personnel. One technician executes the procedure; a second
> acts as safety observer and torque-witness. Both personnel must hold
> ATA Chapter 32 task authorization on type. Estimated task duration:
> 4.5 hours including bleed and leak-check. Schedule hydraulic system
> availability accordingly.
